Welcome to the Semigroups Network (an EMS Topical Activity Group).

The purpose of the Semigroups Network is to bring together researchers in semigroup theory and related areas, by building on existing local networks and providing mechanisms for more isolated researchers to connect with others. We aim to:

  • connect and promote interactions between researchers working in semigroup theory and closely related topics
  • provide a central hub of information for semigroup theorists to help raise awareness of activities within our subject and aid individual researchers looking to connect with those with similar interests
  • organise activities, including meetings and workshops
  • plan, organise, and support applications for European funding programs.

If you would like to join the Semigroups Network, are looking for researchers in semigroup theory to connect with or events for researchers in semigroup theory, then you are in the right place.

If you have any questions about the network or would like to share information about upcoming events/activities/opportunities of interest to researchers in semigroup theory, then please contact us.
